## Full description (max 4000)

```
Timebox Guard puts one small, deliberate step between you and the apps that
eat your day.

When you open an app you've chosen to guard, Timebox Guard asks two
questions before it lets you in:

  • How many minutes do you actually need?
  • What for?

You type a real reason and pick a duration. When the time runs out, the
prompt comes back. It's gentle friction — enough to turn an autopilot tap
into a decision.

There's also a "Close app instead" button on every prompt. Tapping it is a
small win, and the app keeps score.

USAGE METRICS

The home screen is a private dashboard built from your own choices:

  • Focused time in each guarded app, per day, as a bar chart
  • Sessions started vs. times you backed out ("restraint rate")
  • Planned time vs. time actually spent
  • The reasons you've been giving yourself, newest first

Pick a window — today, 7 days, 30 days, all time — or wipe the log with one
tap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

  • No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything stays on your phone. Cloud backup is turned off so the log
    isn't even copied to Google Drive.

HOW IT WORKS

Timebox Guard uses Android's Accessibility Service for a single purpose: to
notice which app you've just opened, so it can show the prompt for guarded
apps and time your sessions. It reads only the foreground app's name — never
screen contents, never what you type. You can turn it off any time in
Android Settings.

A "Display over other apps" permission lets the prompt appear on top of the
guarded app.

NOTE: "Close app instead" sends you to the home screen rather than
force-stopping the other app — Android doesn't allow apps to kill each
other. Some manufacturers (Xiaomi, OnePlus, Huawei…) aggressively stop
background services; if prompts stop appearing, add Timebox Guard to your
battery-optimisation allowlist.
```
